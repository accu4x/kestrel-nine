# Places in the Kestrel reach

Stations in the reach are named after birds of Old Earth. The crew of the first ark started
it and nobody has stopped. Minor stops in generated maps draw from the same list. The old regions
(Nyx Verge, Persephone, Orpheus, Aurelian, Vesta) keep names from the Emergent Veil's charts,
which are older than the reach's settlers. Kestrel's Hold fits both, since a kestrel is a bird.

| Place | What it is | Who is there |
|---|---|---|
| **Kestrel Nine** | Cold ringed gas giant, ninth world of the dim star Kestrel. Everything in the reach orbits near it. | Everyone, eventually |
| **Relay Station** | The hub. Docking Bay 7 is where the story starts. | Winter |
| **The Bazaar** | A market of barges tethered hull to hull. Anything is for sale, including silence. | Traders, fixers |
| **Kestrel's Hold** | The reach's main trade station: rickety and run by Consortium middlemen, with a barter piazza, black-market stalls and seedy lodges for off-the-books deals. | Consortium middlemen, traders |
| **Tollgate** | The Consortium's contract house and customs gate, inside Kestrel's Hold. Every legal cargo is stamped here. | Factor Pell |
| **Vesper Array** | Inquisition listening post. Its sensor bubble makes lanes nearby slow and costly to run dark. | Inquisitor Vey |
| **Halden's Reach** | The mining belt: iron, cobalt, and rare lumen crystal. | Foreman Rook, the Union |
| **Persephone Belt** | Halden's older, deeper neighbour. Its shafts sometimes open onto dormant Warden Nodes, and miners leave offerings to keep them "sleeping". Claim disputes are constant. | Miners, Mining Guild |
| **Cinder** | A volcanic ore moon. Hot, rich and unstable. | Contract crews |
| **Marrow** | A derelict habitat ring. Salvage rights are disputed. | Nobody, officially |
| **Aurelian Ring** | A second derelict ring structure (not Marrow), with a fading distress beacon. | Salvagers |
| **Nyx Verge** | A station in a maze of broken freighter hulks and salvageable drives. See its districts below. | Salvagers, traders, Salem's Armada contacts |
| **Orpheus Reach** | Deep ruins with caches of pre-Collapse data. | Relic hunters, Academia |
| **Vesta Cluster** | Fast debris fields and unpredictable micro-jumps, with hidden caches for anyone who can thread it. | Daredevil pilots, smugglers |
| **The Lantern** | The Augmented safe house. On no chart. | Unknown |
| **The Quiet** | The outer edge where signals fade. Where the Purge has been seen. At its edge the Outer Relay Commune hears the Pale Signal, a transmission that corrupts logs and brings hallucinations. Still unexplained. | The Outer Relay Commune, at the edge |
| **Primavara** | Outside the reach: at the centre of every old chart and redacted from all of them. Lost at the Last Stand. | — |

## Nyx Verge districts

- **The Ring Market:** docking-ring traders, refuelling, basic repairs and job postings.
- **The Spindle:** the salvage yard. Salvage contracts, refit bays, black-market dealers.
- **Glass Quay:** high-end trade, rare artifact auctions, legal brokers.
- **The Signal Array:** relay beacons, comms and telemetry decoding.
- **The Neural Lab:** the one sanctioned augmentation service in the reach.
- **The Black Dock:** unregulated repairs, pirate contacts, forged logs. The Inquisition can't
  enforce much here.

## Warden Nodes

Dormant defence AIs left in ruins, which may wake when disturbed. Before the Glass Accord, the
Wardens were the crews formed to guard them. The Accord made those crews its enforcers, and they
kept the name.

## Units

- **Distance** along lanes: light-hours (**lh**).
- **Ore:** tonnes (**t**).
- **Jammer power:** kilowatt-cycles (**kc**).
- **Treaty support:** **accord** points, the weighted demands a delegation accepts.
