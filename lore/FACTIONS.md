# Factions

## The Augmented

Human minds living in machine frames: ships, lifters, station systems. Outlawed since the
Council of Vesper (Cycle 31). They survive by hiding in plain sight as licensed pilots, and by
leaving coded **Ship's Logs** for each other in the Lantern network.

- **Creed:** Sato's maxim. *The machine is fast. The mind is wide. Neither is enough.*
- **Wants:** to be left alone, to find each other, to help whoever is in trouble.
- **Method:** mutual aid, secrecy, patience. They do not fight unless someone is trapped.
- **In play:** the player is Augmented. Reputation with them rises when you help, share and
  leave good logs.
- **Two lines, one secret.** *Chip-born* minds are echoes recovered from ancient ARK memory
  chips and woken illegally; many carry only fragments of who they were. *Crossers* chose it,
  following Ione Sato. A chip-born mind can be restored from a backup held at a relay, but a
  mind can run only once: a backup stays inert until its mind is lost, and it wakes without
  anything that happened since. `[amended 2026-10-01, lore interview round 5: replaces "and
  every copy asks which one is *you*"; Dan: "your soul is electronic but it cannot clone itself"]`
- **Covert agents.** The Lantern runs covert agents inside every faction. `[added 2026-10-01,
  lore interview round 5]`
- **Captain-words.** A Ship's Log may carry one hidden word: a single ordinary word that other
  Augmented read as a marker for salvage, danger or a safe meeting. Never a name, never
  coordinates.

## The Consortium

The trade monopoly that holds the charter to certify every engine under the Glass Accord.
Every NAV unit, every lift frame, every customs scanner is Consortium glass.

- **Wants:** throughput. Cargo moving, tariffs paid, contracts honored.
- **Believes:** a certified machine with an obedient pilot is enough.
- **Toward the Augmented:** a threat to the certification monopoly, or an asset with unusually
  good numbers. It depends on the quarter.
- **Face in Kestrel:** Factor Oduya Pell, at Tollgate.

## The Galactic Inquisition

Rose across the Emergent Veil in the Long Vigil after the Purge War, to police the ban on
emergent AI. In the Kestrel reach, the Glass Accord's Wardens joined it at the Council of Vesper
(Cycle 31). They hold that a deliberating machine is a sin and a beacon: that the Purge hunts
minds in machines.

- **Wants:** the Accord kept, the Augmented ended, the Purge given nothing to find.
- **The irony they live with:** every Inquisition cutter flies on Consortium glass. They cannot
  hunt machines without machines.
- **Rules of engagement:** they disable and seize. They rarely destroy. They take prisoners.
- **Face in Kestrel:** Inquisitor Halvard Vey, commanding the Vesper Array.

## The Purge

The enemy of the Purge War (NC 1990–2190): a swarm of drones bound to one hive mind, driven
back at the Last Stand at Primavara. In the Kestrel reach it returned in the First Silence. It has
never spoken. Its only signal is a clean, repeating count.

- **Theories:** Academia holds, from Archon relics, that it began as the Archons'
  caretaker-machines. Popular rumor since the Engine Panic says it was a human-built mind. Neither
  is confirmed.
- **Told, not proven:** pilots tell of the Black Tide and the Ash Rains, colonies whose own
  terraformers and climate governors were turned against them. Academia doubts the stories.

- **Known:** it erases life that travels between stars. It ignores life that stays on worlds.
- **Observed:** it sweeps. It never hesitates. It has never been seen to double back.
- **Unknown:** everything else. See `WORLD.md`, *What is deliberately unknown*.

## The Mining Guild

A Veil-wide cartel that holds mining rights and sets ore prices. Ruthless toward unsanctioned
mining. In Kestrel it owns the claims that the Miners' Union works.

- **Face in Kestrel:** Toma Vell, a foreman whose crews matter to him, but whose Guild comes first.

## The Miners' Union (Halden's Reach)

Independent crews working Mining Guild claims in the belt, under Consortium contracts they did
not write. Loyal to each other first.

- **Wants:** fair rates, salvage rights, escorts through the outer belt.
- **Distrusts:** the Consortium (for the contracts) and the Inquisition (for the inspections).
- **Face in Kestrel:** Foreman Tamsin Rook.

## Academia

Scholars of relics, ARK chips and pre-Collapse data. They work at the grey edge of the law, since
some of their revival projects are exactly what the Inquisition hunts.

- **Believes:** the Archon theory of the Purge (see `WORLD.md`), from the relics.
- **Method:** trades in information and artifacts, funds salvage, keeps a careful distance from
  anything visibly Augmented.
- **Face in Kestrel:** Professor Laplace, at the Neural Lab, Nyx Verge.

## Salem's Armada

A piracy ring founded by Salem and led now by her great-granddaughter Selena. It runs like a
matriarchal mafia, and its scouts are rumored to be precognitive. It ambushes salvage crews,
sells black-market services, and sometimes sides against the Inquisition.

- **Rules of engagement:** genuinely dangerous. The Armada is the one faction in the reach that
  kills, the exception that makes the rest of the reach feel safer. The pilot's own combat still
  disables and escapes.

## Black Market Purveyors

Dealers in illicit modules, illegal AI firmware and archeotech. The only source of some rare gear,
at a price.

## The Outer Relay Commune

Relay operators and data-scratchers at the edges of the network, suspicious of any central
authority. They watch the Quiet: their relays are the ones going dark, and they know more about
the Quiet than anyone.
